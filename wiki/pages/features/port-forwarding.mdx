# Port Forwarding Hub

k7s provides a full-featured **Port Forwarding Hub** for routing local TCP traffic directly to Pods and Services running inside your Kubernetes cluster.

---

## Creating & Managing Forwards

### 1. From Pods or Services
- In any Pod or Service detail panel or context menu, click **Port Forward…**.
- Select target container port, choose a local port (or auto-assign a free random port), and click **Start Forward**.

### 2. The Forwards Management Workspace
- View all active and configured port-forwards across all clusters in a single dashboard.
- Real-time connection status indicators (`running`, `connecting`, `stopped`, `error`).
- **One-Click Browser Launch:** Click the link or open icon to launch `http://localhost:<port>` directly in your default browser.
- **Reconnect Presets:** Save frequently used port-forward configurations as named presets to restart them in a single click whenever you reconnect to the cluster.
